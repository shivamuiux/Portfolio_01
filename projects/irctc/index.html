Overview

Redesigning IRCTC Rail Connect to refine the train booking journey and resolve usability issues

The Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation's (IRCTC) Rail Connect mobile app is the preferred method for purchasing train tickets in India. It is one of the most transacted websites in Asia - Pacific with a transaction volume of over 25 million per month and 5.5 mn logins per day. On an average 12.38 lakhs tickets were sold daily through IRCTC's website and mobile app during FY 2023-24

But despite all of its features, a lot of customers choose to use third-party apps instead of the official app because they don't like the poor user experience. Realising this, I made the decision to address these issues with a redesign that would improve usability and the user experience in general. My objective was to design an interface that would satisfy the needs of contemporary users by being more smooth and intuitive.

Research

The Starting Point - Putting Users at the
center my work

To gain a comprehensive understanding and gather valuable insights about the IRCTC Mobile App, I conducted user research. I started by reaching out to friends, relatives, and professors who regularly travel by train to share their experiences with the app. Additionally, I sought feedback from my father, who, through his work in the Railway Protection Force (RPF), has a deep connection with the railways, offering unique insights into the app's functionality.

However, to broaden my perspective, I placed significant emphasis on analyzing user reviews from Google Play and the Apple App Store. These reviews provided a wealth of feedback directly from the app's users, highlighting common pain points, suggestions for improvements, and general sentiment. The insights gathered from these reviews were invaluable in understanding the real-world challenges users face when interacting with the app.

Inconsistent Navigation

Users face challenges as a result of irregular screen layouts and nonstandard navigation elements.

Overwhelming Information
Density

The application presents an excessive amount of information at once, which confuses users and makes it difficult for them to locate pertinent details.

Edit Passenger Info

Recognition Difficulties

Key takeaway

This redesign helped me understand how small usability issues can have a significant impact on the overall experience. By combining user feedback, app reviews, and my own exploration, I learned to look beyond visual improvements and focus on making the experience clearer, simpler, and more intuitive for users.

More than redesigning an interface, this project taught me to question existing design decisions and approach problems from the user's perspective.
